# Vehicle Model

## 1. Overview

The Vehicle model calculates the main longitudinal forces and power requirements of the Smart EV Powertrain Simulator.

The calculations include:

- Rolling resistance force
- Aerodynamic drag force
- Total resistance force
- Required cruising motor power
- Acceleration
- Acceleration force
- Total tractive force
- Peak mechanical power
